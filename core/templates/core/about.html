About Ubumenyi Hub

Preserving Rwandan culture through accessible, bilingual, offline-first learning.

Our Mission

Ubumenyi Hub exists to make Rwandan culture learnable, shareable, and verifiable — for every Rwandan, in Rwanda and abroad. We believe culture is a living library. Our platform keeps it alive online and offline, in Kinyarwanda and English.

What We Teach

  • Language — Kinyarwanda reading, writing, proverbs (imigani), idioms.
  • History — Pre-colonial kingdoms, colonial era, independence, unity and reconciliation.
  • Arts & Music — Intore dance, ingoma drumming, crafts, poetry.
  • Heritage & Environment — Sacred sites, national parks, traditional food, ceremonies.

Our Values

  • Accessibility — Offline-first so learning reaches every corner of Rwanda.
  • Bilingualism — Every lesson in Kinyarwanda and English.
  • Trust — Blockchain-style verifiable certificates.
  • Respect — Content reviewed with elders, historians, and cultural institutions.

Closing Note

Ubumenyi ni Ubusenge. Knowledge is Light. Together, we carry Rwanda’s culture forward.
